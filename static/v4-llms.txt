- [StateView](https://docs.ring.exchange/contracts/v4/guides/state-view): Read pool state using StateView for offchain and onchain integrations.
- [Unlock Callback](https://docs.ring.exchange/contracts/v4/guides/unlock-callback): Understand unlock-based execution and settlement deltas.
- [Custom Accounting](https://docs.ring.exchange/contracts/v4/guides/custom-accounting): Extend pool behavior with custom accounting logic.
- [Swap Routing](https://docs.ring.exchange/contracts/v4/guides/swap-routing): Route swaps across pools and routers in the current v4 integration stack.
