Updated: October 30, 2026

We are ending support for Blast (chain ID `81457`) in the Ring interface.

## What is changing?

Blast will be removed from the Ring interface's supported networks. Swaps, liquidity management, staking, and wrapping
on Blast will no longer be available through the interface.

## Why is this changing?

Blast has announced that it is shutting down. We are ending interface support in response to this decision.

## What do I need to do?

Use a supported network to continue using [Ring](https://app.ring.exchange/). For supported assets held in your Blast
wallet, follow the [official withdrawal instructions](https://docs.blast.io/withdrawals/withdraw-to-ethereum).

If your application links to Ring on Blast, remove Blast-specific links and network options from your Ring integration.
